May this frontend be useful, clear, and calm.

May it serve the people who rely on it,
and not burden them with needless friction.

May notifications arrive when they should,
stay quiet when they should,
and never distract more than they help.

May the code remain readable to the next person,
including the one who wrote it and returns later.

May changes be made with care,
bugs be found before users find them,
and fixes be smaller than the failures they correct.

May this project grow only as complex as it must,
and no more.

May operators have good logs,
developers have good tests,
and users have a stable interface.

May we be honest about what is unfinished,
careful with what is trusted,
and disciplined about what is exposed.

May this work do more good than harm.
